A Large Language Model (LLM):
       is a transformer-based AI model trained on massive text datasets to understand and generate human language 
by predicting the next token in a sequence

input and encoding :
Input Encoding means converting human-readable text into a numerical format that the model can understand and process.

Transformer:
A Transformer is a neural network architecture that uses self-attention mechanisms to process and understand relationships 
between words in parallel, enabling efficient training and high-quality language understanding and generation.

vactor embeddings:
A vector embedding is a dense numerical representation of text, words, sentences, or documents in a high-dimensional vector space. 
It captures semantic meaning so that similar concepts are located close together and dissimilar concepts are farther apart.
